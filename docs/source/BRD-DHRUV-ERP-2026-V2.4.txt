    Statutory Table Headers: The quotation table explicitly specifies Sl No , Description of Goods , HSN / SAC
    Code , Pack Size , Quantity (Units) , Rate (₹) , CGST % , SGST % , and Amount (₹) .

    A4 Print Preview Modal: Instant print frame with formal corporate header, bank details, 7-day validity banner,
    itemized tax ledger breakdown, and authorized signature blocks.
  5.2 Invoice Engine (Statutory Tax Invoice)
    Tax Invoice Generation: Generates statutory commercial invoices complying with Indian GST rules (CGST +
    SGST for intra-state Maharashtra transactions; IGST for inter-state supply).
    E-Way Bill Integration: Formatted fields for 12-digit Government E-Way Bill Number, Vehicle Number, and E-
    Way Expiry Date.
    Statutory Rounding: Auto-calculation of round-off paise to ensure exact rupee totals.

   Production storekeepers cannot issue quarantined material to manufacturing lines until the QC Manager
   digitally releases the batch with an approved Certificate of Analysis (CoA).
